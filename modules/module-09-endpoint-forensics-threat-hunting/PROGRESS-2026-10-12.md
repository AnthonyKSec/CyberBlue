## Security principle and demonstrated capability

Endpoint telemetry is only useful after the investigator proves trusted client enrollment, reliable collection, evidence interpretation, and controlled transport between network trust zones. The collection server must keep management access private, accept only authorized client connections, and preserve its forensic datastore through service restarts. A green connection indicator is not a substitute for an actual collection result.
